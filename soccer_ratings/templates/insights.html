About ratings1x2

ratings1x2 is a premium soccer analytics dashboard offering comprehensive home and away ratings for major leagues worldwide. We provide historical ratings, round-by-round fixture projections, and implied match odds (1X2, Draw No Bet, Over/Under, and Both Teams to Score) derived using Elo and decay calibration sweeps.
